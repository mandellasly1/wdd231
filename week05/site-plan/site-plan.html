Sankofa Journeys

A site plan for the Sankofa Journeys website

Site Name

Site Name: Sankofa Journeys

Description: Sankofa Journeys is a cultural and educational platform dedicated to celebrating African heritage and ancestral exploration. The name reflects the company's focus on the importance of remembering and honoring one's roots, as symbolized by the Sankofa bird, which teaches us to look back to move forward.

Site Purpose

Purpose: The website will serve as a hub for cultural and educational resources, providing information about African heritage, ancestral stories, and community events. It will also offer a platform for sharing personal journeys and connecting with others who are exploring their roots.

Services and Information: The site will provide articles, videos, and multimedia content about African culture and history, a community forum for sharing stories, and a calendar of cultural events. It will also feature a resource library with books, documentaries, and other materials for learning and exploration.

Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: What cultural events are happening in my community?
  • Scenario 2: How can I learn more about my ancestral heritage?

Color Schema

  • Primary Color: #8B4513 (Saddle Brown) - Used for headings and buttons to convey trust and energy.
  • Secondary Color: #CC5500 (Burnt Orange) - Used for accents and highlights to draw attention.
  • Accent Color: #A0522D (Deep Terracotta) - Used for borders and icons to add depth.
  • Background Color: #F5F5DC (Beige) - Used for the main background to create a warm and inviting atmosphere.
  • Text Color: #333333 (Charcoal Gray) - Used for body text to ensure readability.
  • Muted Text Color: #666666 (Muted Gray) - Used for secondary text to provide contrast.

Typography

  • Primary Font: Arial, sans-serif - Used for body text for readability and a clean look.
  • Secondary Font: Roboto, sans-serif - Used for headings to add a modern and professional feel.
